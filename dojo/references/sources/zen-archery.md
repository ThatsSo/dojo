# Zen in the Art of Archery (Eugen Herrigel, 1948)

> Status: seed (2026-11-07), copied from the summary in `sources.md`. To be expanded from James's write-up (`writeups/zen-archery.md`).

## Core idea
- A long foundation of repetition before the "real" target; breathing to remove strain.
- The release should happen by itself, not be forced; the result is judged by process and state of mind rather than hits.
- Faking a technique to imitate mastery blocks real mastery.
- Patience through plateaus; learning by watching and copying the master; quieting the analytical mind.
